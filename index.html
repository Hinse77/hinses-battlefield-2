    ARENA FIELD GUIDE

    Know the battlefield.

    Your objective

    Reach 40,000 mass before the ten-minute timer expires. On Very Hard you must also take arena rank #1: reaching the target starts the final Conquest Phase. At time up, the largest organism wins. You can absorb organisms that are at least 15% smaller and mostly inside your orb.

    Rank points: target progress, fast finishes, combos, wins, boss defeats and genuine top-two end duels. Toxic poison reduces both mass and rank points.

    Move & survive

    Mouse: move toward cursor; triple-click one direction for Overdrive. Keyboard: WASD / arrows; Q activates Overdrive. Mobile: virtual stick and OVERDRIVE button.

    Space pauses. Overdrive lasts 5 seconds, then recharges for 18 seconds — or 15 seconds as Interceptor. The Arena Radar shows you, the ten strongest rivals and every active boss. Avoid larger orbs and use food routes to grow safely.

    Organism types

    • Toxic Crazer — poison core; absorbing it costs the eater mass. On Hard and Very Hard it converts particles into extra growth.
    • Coward — survival scavenger; reads multiple threats, chooses safer food routes and escapes with Panic Dash.
    • Hunter — aggressive Bloodrush pursuer.
    • Opportunist — larger starter, quick ambushes.
    • Chaotic — wild movement; its burning fuse warns of a damaging shockwave. Keep clear.
    • Elite — smart Vector Burst; during Elite Recovery it abandons hopeless attacks, avoids guarded prey and hunts valuable mid-sized targets. Its comeback growth fades once it reaches the arena lead.

    Boss protocol

    Bosses have an orbit ring; it brightens while an ability is active. Titan Vex charges, Ember Nyx bursts, and Void Orion opens a Void Rift that empowers hunters and elites.

    Toxic Master fires green Adaptive Venom arrows. They are gentler against small targets, scale up against massive targets, and siphon part of the damage back to him. He is poisonous too: absorbing him costs mass. If the arena AI defeats a boss, its Boss Core can restore it once after 20 seconds; a boss defeated by you stays down. Hard has Titan Vex and Toxic Master; very hard has all four bosses.

    Combat doctrines

    Interceptor: 12% faster movement and a shorter Overdrive cooldown. Assimilator: 45% more particle growth and 12% more safe absorption growth. Aegis: 35% less venom, toxic-core and shockwave damage.

    Your doctrine is saved, shown in the HUD, and included in Battlefield 2 balance telemetry.

    Difficulty

    Easy: more prey and a larger start. Normal: balanced arena. Hard: more hunters and two bosses. Very hard: smarter opponents, all four bosses and a Toxic Crazer charge.

    On Hard and Very Hard, your progress drives the response: at 50% mass the arena watches you, at 75% Elites enter tactical recovery, and at 90% the full arena alarm begins. Time pressure also advances these stages if a round runs long.

    Challenge a friend

    Finish a round and choose Challenge a friend. On mobile, the share menu opens; on desktop, your result, points, arena code and game link are copied together. Copy arena code remains available for the short version.

    Entering the code on the start screen selects the difficulty and creates the same starting food, opponents and bosses. The arena is shared, not scripted: movement, abilities and the final score still depend on each player.

    Service ranks

    Earn permanent service points across every completed round. Difficulty adjusts the reward: Easy 70%, Normal 100%, Hard 125%, Very Hard 160%. Your military badge is displayed directly on your orb; the global service board recognizes players by arena name.

      Tip: chain quick absorptions to build combos, but leave toxic green Crazers alone. Copy an arena code after a round and enter it on the start screen to challenge friends on the same arena.
